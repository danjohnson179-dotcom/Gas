ABOUT THE DATA

Transparent by design

Electricity

Live generation and demand are requested from Elexon's public Insights API. No API key is embedded in this website.

Wholesale prices

Electricity uses Elexon's Market Index Data. Gas links directly to National Gas's current Gas Day market information.

Important

Live energy datasets can be delayed, revised or temporarily unavailable. The dashboard shows a clear data status instead of fabricating values.
