ABOUT

Simple, transparent energy data.

Electricity

Demand, generation and short-term wholesale market data come from Elexon.

Gas

The gas card uses a UK NBP benchmark and converts pence per therm into GBP/MWh.

Grid Pulse

Grid Pulse is a simple dashboard score based on demand, low-carbon generation and wholesale electricity price. It is an interpretation aid, not an official grid metric.
