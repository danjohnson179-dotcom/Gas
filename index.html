ABOUT THE DATA

Transparent by design

Electricity

Generation, demand and market-index prices are requested from Elexon's public Insights service.

Gas

National Gas is the source for the GB gas market figure. Its pence-per-therm price is converted to GBP/MWh so it can be compared directly with wholesale electricity.

Data quality

Energy datasets can be delayed or temporarily unavailable. This site shows feed status instead of inventing missing live values.
